## Tests requiring other hardware (not available)

- [ ] **A capture card (or a camera driver) with no signal**: check the video hang fix (code done 2026-11-24, `QUESTIONS.md` REV-2); the MSI camera-off test (item 6) is only an approximation. Device: any capture card or virtual camera whose driver blocks in `ReadSample` when nothing is plugged in. Expected: the open runs on its worker with a message (8 s wait), the drop shuts the source down and waits at most 1.5 s, the output window never freezes, and the picture of a live card is unchanged.

Device: a capture card or camera that delivers RGB32 without the attribute. Expected: the picture is the right way up; if the device turns out to deliver it top-down, keep the top-down reading for it.

## Potential ShaderGlass gaps to fulfill

From the comparison of 2026-11-25 (`QUESTIONS.md`, "Comparison with ShaderGlass": its public pages only, nothing measured). Each entry is tagged **gap** (ShaderGlass does it, Phoxium does not), **check** (probably covered or a doubt to verify), **decision** (the owner chooses a direction) or **by design** (not planned, to confirm). The project's differentiator is the physical model, not the shader library, so most of these are the owner's call.

- [ ] **gap, decision: an overlay without choosing a window (Desktop Glass).** A transparent window that shades whatever is behind it. Phoxium captures a window or a whole monitor and has click-through; the pieces of an overlay exist (the output window is already hidden from screen captures when a whole monitor is the source, the click-through option passes the mouse), what is missing is a region source: capture the screen area behind the output window, with the output excluded from its own capture. Decide whether it is a goal; if so, a design note first (the focus rules, the moving and resizing of the region, the cost of capturing the monitor).
- [ ] **gap: motion clarity at high refresh (black frame insertion, a rolling beam scan).** ShaderGlass had black frame insertion in its alpha builds and moved it, with a CRT beam simulator, to a separate app (ShaderBeam). Phoxium models the afterglow (persistence) but not the scan: on a 240 or 300 Hz panel a rolling scan of the beam at the refresh rate would be a physical feature, not an effect. First measure what the MSI 300 Hz panel shows with the current persistence (MSI item 4), then decide.
- [ ] **gap: hardware and system reach.** ShaderGlass needs a DirectX 11 GPU and Windows 10 2004 or later (and runs on Linux through Wine); Phoxium needs Vulkan 1.3 and states Windows 11. Close the doubts with the MSI items 2 and 8 (a UHD 630, an NVIDIA GeForce driver, Windows 10 if it is installed there) and the AMD item (other hardware); relax the README only on what was tested. A DirectX back end is not planned (Vulkan is decided).
- [ ] **gap: a video device from the command line.** ShaderGlass takes `-input`; Phoxium has no flag to choose a capture card or a camera at start (only the picker). Check how the picker names devices, then add `--video <name>` (the matching rule of `--window`) so a launcher can start a capture-card session without the picker.
- [ ] **check: the output scale below 100 % and a stretch (new in ShaderGlass 1.3.2).** Probably covered: `--aspect fill` or a forced ratio stretches the picture, `--scale-mode manual` with a fractional `source_pixel_size` and any output window size scale it down. Verify on the development PC (a 640 x 480 source in a 300 x 225 window, a stretch to 16:9) and note the answer in `REFERENCE.md`.
- [ ] **check: a dedicated GPU that is not recognised, a multi-monitor mode (their #309 and #310).** Phoxium chooses the adapter of the monitor the output covers or the `[global.gpu]` setting. Done on the development PC; the MSI items 2 and 3 cover the Optimus and the Discrete modes.
- [ ] **check: Windows AutoHDR content over-exposed (their itch.io comments).** Test a capture of an AutoHDR-lit window or game with Windows HDR on: Phoxium outputs SDR, so the picture must not be clipped or washed out. Needs an HDR monitor (the HDR item under other hardware).
- [ ] **check: the mouse pointer stuck on the screen (their #299).** Hand-check the pointer after F10, F11, Alt+Tab, a quit, a crash of the source, and in click-through (Phoxium hides the host cursor over the output while the menu is closed). About 10 minutes at the development PC.
- [ ] **decision: reach and distribution.** ShaderGlass is on Steam and as a zip, with a large user base that finds the problems; Phoxium is a GitHub zip, unsigned (the SmartScreen warning). A Steam page or itch.io would give reach and a way past the warning; it ties to signing (Very low) and to the owner's wish for users.
- [ ] **by design, to confirm: importing external shaders (the 1200+ libretro slang shaders).** Phoxium is one physical model with parameters in real units; a shader player is another product. Not planned. Also not to take: code or shaders (ShaderGlass is GPL-3.0, its shaders carry their own licences; the policy is inspiration only).
