## Source colour targets (new feature, one line per platform)

The idea (owner, 2026-11-25): the chain stays as it is; a step at its head turns the colours of the source into what the device it came from would have shown, as accurately as the sources allow, before the beam and the mask render it. **Built:** the slot `target` (`source_colour_target`, per-game profile fields `colour_target`, `colour_variant`), four transform kinds (`matrix`, `curve`, `palette`, `dac`), the data-file format with a provenance per value (`presets/targets/`, `src/targets.rs`), the oracle (`crt_model::target`) and a GPU test per target, the 5-bit codes recovered exactly from expanded pixels. **The research:** every platform is audited in `QUESTIONS.md` ("Audit of the colour targets": data, verdict, transform kind, licence), seven independent audits; no target ships without a named source for its numbers. Licence rule: numbers and measurements with attribution, never code or tables from GPL, LGPL or AGPL repositories.

- [ ] **WonderSwan Color and SwanCrystal**: the matrix (R 26/4/2, G 0/24/8, B 6/4/22) is the Game Boy Color matrix of higan carried over, and ares applies it to the WSC too; the SwanCrystal panel is a Citizen reflective TFT (the GBC panel is Sharp, so the analogy is weak), the WSC an FSTN colour LCD with a dial and a High/Low contrast mode. A heuristic only, labelled unmeasured.
- [ ] **Apple II (NTSC composite)**: the output is now documented (aux pin black about 0.75 V, white about 2.0 V into 470 ohm; the jack about 1 V into 75 ohm; the burst resistor R6 2.7k; the 14M delay of 90 degrees); the colours stay derived (Munafo, Ticmanis, the emulators). A signal-driven target: square-wave bit patterns on the 45-degree grid, burst reference, hue and chroma as user trims; the decode constants of OpenEmulator and AppleWin exist as numbers (GPL: attribution only). Missing: the summing resistors R3 to R15, the burst in IRE, the chroma ratio and hue offsets.
- [ ] **ZX Spectrum**: still no per-colour Y, U, V; the original documents are known (Amstrad's ULA patent EP0107687B1, the Ferranti ULA 6C001 and 5C102 specifications, a TEA1002 datasheet and the Issue 2 schematic on archive.org) but are image-only PDFs. Derived and low-confidence: an ideal PAL encoding of the 3-bit RGB, normal to bright about 0.85, saturation a user parameter; the +2A and +3 RGB socket (1.67 V p-p) computed.

Nothing defensible (an identity target at most, labelled "emulator convention, unmeasured"; they need a measurement of a real unit or a hand-read source):

- [ ] **Atari Lynx**: no data; Handy and the libretro core scale each 4-bit channel by 17 and offer only a ghosting filter; the panel is three 4-bit latches per pixel, the level curve sits in the LCD module (not a Mikey DAC); the Lynx II backlight type is unconfirmed.
- [ ] **Neo Geo Pocket Color**: no data; no emulator or shader has NGPC-specific colour numbers (reflective TFT, 160 x 152, no backlight; ares scales linearly).
- [ ] **Sega Game Gear**: no data; Genesis Plus GX duplicates each nibble (x17) and has an off-by-default ghosting filter at 0.80 (origin undocumented); MAME's persistence hack is on by default; panel type unconfirmed.
